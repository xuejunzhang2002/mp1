- Polar Bears International, polar bear fact pages (diet, denning, cubs, anatomy)
- Pagano et al. (2012), "Long-distance swimming by polar bears (*Ursus maritimus*) of the southern Beaufort Sea during years of extensive open water", *Canadian Journal of Zoology* (687 km swim)
